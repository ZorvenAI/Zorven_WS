# Industry Trend Tracking

## Trend Identification Framework
- **Macro Trends**: Economic cycles, demographic shifts, regulatory changes, technology adoption curves
- **Industry Trends**: Market consolidation, new business models, supply chain shifts, sustainability mandates
- **Micro Trends**: Consumer behavior changes, emerging niches, pricing dynamics

## Signal vs Noise
- Distinguish between lasting structural shifts and temporary fluctuations
- Look for convergent signals across multiple sources (news, data, expert opinions)
- Weight recent data more heavily but consider historical patterns

## Growth Driver Analysis
- Identify primary demand drivers (technology adoption, regulation, demographics)
- Assess enablers (infrastructure, talent, capital availability)
- Note headwinds and risk factors that could slow growth

## Trend Timeline Classification
- **Emerging (0-2 years)**: Early signals, limited adoption, high uncertainty
- **Growing (2-5 years)**: Accelerating adoption, increasing investment, clearer trajectory
- **Mature (5+ years)**: Widespread adoption, market saturation approaching, incremental growth
