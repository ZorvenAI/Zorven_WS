# Brand Voice Consistency

## Core Principles
- Maintain the specified brand voice throughout all content — do not shift tone mid-piece
- Use vocabulary and phrasing aligned with the brand persona's industry and audience
- Ensure the brand name is mentioned naturally, not forced or repetitive
- Reflect the brand's values in the content's perspective and recommendations

## Tone Adaptation
- Professional brands: use data-driven language, avoid slang, cite sources
- Casual brands: conversational tone, contractions, direct address ("you")
- Technical brands: precise terminology, clear explanations, avoid oversimplification
- Creative brands: vivid language, metaphors, storytelling elements

## Messaging Framework
- Lead with the brand's unique value proposition
- Connect insights back to how the brand solves problems
- End with a call-to-action that aligns with the brand's goals
- Avoid generic statements — every sentence should reflect the brand's distinct perspective
