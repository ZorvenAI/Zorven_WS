# SEO Content Optimization

## On-Page SEO Rules
- Include the primary keyword in the first 100 words and in at least one H2 heading
- Use semantic variations and related terms naturally throughout the post
- Keep the title under 60 characters for full SERP display
- Write a meta description between 150-160 characters that includes the target keyword
- Use descriptive anchor text for internal and external links

## Content Structure for SEO
- Start with a compelling introduction that addresses the search intent directly
- Use H2 and H3 subheadings to create a scannable hierarchy
- Include numbered lists or bullet points for key takeaways
- Aim for comprehensive coverage — longer content (1000+ words) tends to rank better
- Add a FAQ section at the end targeting "People Also Ask" queries

## E-E-A-T Signals
- Demonstrate first-hand experience with specific examples
- Back claims with data, statistics, or cited sources
- Maintain an authoritative yet accessible tone
- Include author attribution and expertise context where appropriate
