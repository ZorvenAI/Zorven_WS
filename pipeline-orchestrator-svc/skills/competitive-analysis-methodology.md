# Competitive Analysis Methodology

## ISO 10668 Framework
- Apply the Royalty Relief method for brand valuation when financial data is available
- Calculate Brand Strength Index (BSI) across all pillars before determining royalty rates
- Use industry-specific royalty rate benchmarks as the baseline
- Factor in both quantitative metrics and qualitative brand attributes

## Competitive Gap Analysis
- Identify at least 3-5 direct competitors in the same market segment
- Evaluate competitors across the same dimensions used for the target brand
- Quantify gaps as percentage differences, not just qualitative statements
- Distinguish between capability gaps (fixable) and structural gaps (market-driven)
- Prioritize gaps by business impact and feasibility of closing them

## Actionable Recommendations
- Link each recommendation to a specific identified gap
- Provide both quick wins (0-3 months) and strategic initiatives (6-12 months)
- Include estimated effort or investment level for each recommendation
- Rank recommendations by expected ROI or competitive impact
