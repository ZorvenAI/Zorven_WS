## Competitive Positioning Strategy

### Positioning Map Construction
Build a 2D positioning map using the most relevant axes for the industry:
- **Common axes**: Price vs. Feature richness, Enterprise vs. SMB focus, Self-serve vs. Sales-led, Breadth vs. Depth
- Plot each competitor based on evidence from profiling data
- Identify clusters and outliers

### Gap Identification Framework
For each dimension, identify gaps where no competitor has strong presence:
1. **Unserved segments** — customer types no one targets well
2. **Feature gaps** — capabilities no competitor offers
3. **Price gaps** — underserved price points
4. **Geographic gaps** — regions with limited competition
5. **Channel gaps** — distribution methods not utilized

### Opportunity Scoring
Rate each gap on a 0-10 scale based on:
- **Market attractiveness** (size of unserved demand)
- **Feasibility** (how easily can the gap be filled)
- **Defensibility** (can a moat be built)
- **Alignment** (fit with the brand's strengths)

### Differentiation Dimensions
Analyze competitors across standard differentiation levers:
- Product features and technology
- Pricing strategy and packaging
- Customer experience and support quality
- Brand perception and trust
- Go-to-market approach
- Integration ecosystem
