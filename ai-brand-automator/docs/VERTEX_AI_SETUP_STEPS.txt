TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Error: "Discovery Engine API has not been used in project"
→ Complete Step 1 (Enable the API)

Error: "Permission denied" or "403"
→ Complete Step 2 (Grant Discovery Engine Admin role)

Error: "Data store not found"
→ Complete Step 3 (Create the data store)
